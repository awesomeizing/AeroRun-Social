01

Executive summary

One month to turn a launch into a conversation: show the shoe in real fits, prove it's worth ₹5,999, and let creators and customers do the talking.

28
Creatives: 12 posts, 8 Reels, 5 Story sequences, 3 carousels
4
Content pillars: Fit Check, Light Proof, The Crew, The Drop
5
Micro-creators, ₹25,000 (Project 01 budget) [S9]
≥ 3.0%
Engagement-rate target vs ~2.2% fashion median [S7]

The job. Project 01 found that the 18–25 metro buyer is easy to reach but hesitates over price and fakes. Social media has to do three things paid ads alone can't: make AeroRun look good on real people, answer doubts in public, and build a community whose posts become the proof [S9].

The idea. Light on your feet. Loud on your feed. Every piece of content either shows a fit (style), proves a claim (comfort, value, authenticity) or hands the stage to someone else (creators and customers).

How Instagram decides reach. Instagram's head has named watch time, likes and sends (DMs to friends) as the top ranking signals, and sends matter most for reaching non-followers [S3, S4] Verified. So the content is designed to be sent: colourway personalities, "send this to your Midnight friend", the fake-spotting guide.

What we'll measure. Engagement rate by reach, sends per reach, saves, link clicks and add-to-carts from tagged links, plus UGC volume and response time. A monthly template calculates all of them automatically Recommendation.

Strategist's note

Decision. Set objectives for organic and creator activity only, and pick one per funnel stage.

Alternatives considered. One blended target covering paid + organic; or vanity targets (likes, followers) only.

Why. Blending hides which activity worked. Likes and followers alone say nothing about purchase intent, so each stage has its own KPI, ending in add-to-carts tracked through tagged links. Where no trustworthy benchmark exists (sends per reach), we set our own baseline in week 1 instead of inventing a number.

Four insights carried over from Project 01 [S9]

  • Discovery happens on social, so the feed is the shop window Hypothesis.
  • ₹5,999 is a considered buy, so content must justify the price without discounting Hypothesis.
  • Counterfeit fear is real, so trust content (official store, spotting fakes) is a pillar, not an afterthought Hypothesis.
  • Creators carry trust the brand can't claim for itself, so creators get the Reels, not just reposts Hypothesis.

Designing for the algorithm

Watch time [S3]Hook in the first second; Reels 12–30 sec; captions burned in for muted viewing
Sends per reach [S3, S4]Content made to be DM'd: colourway personalities, fake-spotting guide, 'send to your friend' CTAs
Likes per reach [S3]Strong single-image posts for the follower feed (likes count slightly more with followers)
SavesCarousels (styling, FAQ) and the trust post are designed as reference material

Cadence

One feed item a day on 25 of 30 days, plus Stories every day Assumption. Reels at 8 pm, posts and carousels at 1 pm, until week-1 Insights show when our followers are actually online Assumption.

Strategist's note

Decision. Daily feed posting for the launch month, Reels-heavy (8 of 23 feed items).

Alternatives considered. 3 posts a week (lower workload); Reels only.

Why. A launch month needs momentum, and daily posting lets us test more formats in 30 days. Reels-only would miss saves (carousels) and the strong likes signal of static posts with followers. Cross-format data suggests carousels and Reels both outperform static images on engagement [S8].

Strategist's note

Decision. Keep 'The Drop' (sales and launch moments) to about 10% of the feed.

Alternatives considered. A promotion-heavy launch month (40%+ 'shop now' posts).

Why. A feed full of ads gives people no reason to follow or send it. Style and proof content earns attention, and the paid budget handles direct response.

Hashtag strategy

Fewer, sharper hashtags. They help Instagram categorise a post, not push it further.

The rule has changed. Instagram now caps posts and Reels at 5 hashtags [S1, S2] Verified, and its head has said hashtags help with search but don't increase reach [S1] Verified. Stuffing 30 tags is no longer possible, and wasn't working anyway.

Influencer strategy

Five micro-creators, each chosen for one persona and one job. Honest content over scripted endorsements.

CreatorCityNichePersonaDeliverablesFeeWhy
C1MumbaiStreet-style fashionAaravR03 partnership Reel + 3 Stories₹5,000Owns the Fit Check angle; audience matches Aarav
C2BengaluruWomen's minimal / workwear styleMeeraR04 day-in-the-life Reel + C02 outfit input₹5,000Women-led styling, the gap Meera's persona flagged
C3Delhi NCRSneaker reviewsKabirR05 honest unboxing Reel₹5,000Credibility with sneaker culture; honesty beats hype
C4PuneCampus lifestyleAaravR06 reply Reel + S04 Stories takeover₹5,000Answers real questions in a peer voice
C5HyderabadDance / movementAarav, KabirR07 challenge Reel + original audio₹5,000Remixable format built for sends and UGC

Fees are assumptions until confirmed with quotes Assumption. Total ₹25,000, as in Project 01 [S9].

Selection criteria

  • ≥ 60% of audience aged 18–34 in Indian metros (Insights screenshot)
  • Engagement above their niche's median, with comments that read as real
  • No competitor deal in the last 90 days
  • Brand-safety review of the last 50 posts
  • AeroRun fits their existing style; no forced pivots

The brief, in one page

  • Must: show the shoe on-foot in the first 2 seconds; mention the official store; use #ad
  • Freedom: their own voice, edit and music; honest opinions, including one con if they have one
  • Never: invented specs, discount claims, competitor bashing
  • Rights: 30-day usage for paid partnership ads

Disclosure (ASCI)

India's advertising self-regulator requires paid creator content to carry a clear, prominent disclosure label in each post, such as #ad, Collaboration or Partnership. A bio mention doesn't count, and 2025 updates say the platform's own paid-partnership tag isn't enough on its own [S5, S6] Verified. Brands share responsibility with creators [S6]. So every creator caption in the caption library includes #ad, and gifted nano-creator posts are labelled too Recommendation.

Nano-creator seeding

10–15 gifted pairs to nano-creators (1k–10k followers) with no posting obligation, to seed early #AeroRunFitCheck UGC. The product cost sits outside the ₹1 lakh cash budget Assumption.

Strategist's note

Decision. Use micro-creators as content makers (they shoot the Reels) and run their posts as partnership ads.

Alternatives considered. One larger creator; or creators who only repost brand-made content.

Why. Five creators give five audiences and five content styles to compare. Reposted brand content loses the creator's voice, which is the whole reason followers trust them.

Community management

Fast, human replies are part of the campaign, not after-sales.

Tone of voice

  • Sound like a friend who knows sneakers, not a call centre
  • Short, warm, specific; use the commenter's name or detail
  • Hinglish is fine when the commenter uses it
  • Never argue in public; move problems to DMs
  • Never promise what the product page doesn't (no invented specs or delivery dates)

Response times

TypeStandard
CommentsWithin 2 hours, 10 am–10 pm
DMsWithin 4 hours, 10 am–10 pm
ComplaintsAcknowledge within 1 hour; resolve or update within 24 hours
Crisis signalsEscalate to the brand lead within 1 hour

Response playbook

SituationWhat we doOwner
Praise / fit photosLike + personal reply; ask permission to feature (see UGC flow)Community manager
Product questions (size, comfort, delivery)Reply with the saved answer + link to the FAQ carousel or size guideCommunity manager
Price objections ('too expensive')Acknowledge, don't argue; mention free size exchange and official-store authenticityCommunity manager
Complaints (order, quality)Thank, apologise, move to DM, log the case, follow up in 24 hoursCM → customer care
Counterfeit reports / fake-seller linksThank the person, confirm official channels, report the account with Meta's IP reporting toolsCM → brand lead
Spam, hate, harassmentHide or delete per the moderation policy; block repeat offenders; don't engageCommunity manager
Crisis (trending defect claim, creator controversy)Pause scheduled posts and boosts; holding statement; escalateBrand lead

Saved replies

ForReply
SizingAeroRun runs true to size for most people. Our size guide is in the link in bio, and if it's not right, size exchange is free 🙌
Is it genuine?Genuine AeroRun is sold only at the official store and listed partners (link in bio). If you've seen it much cheaper elsewhere, please send us the link so we can check it.
Too expensiveTotally get it, ₹5,999 is a real decision. That's why every pair comes with free size exchange, and you're buying from the official store, so no fakes.
DeliveryDelivery times depend on your pin code; the product page shows the exact estimate at checkout.
Feature requestLove this, thank you! Can we feature your fit on our page? Reply "Yes AeroRun" and we'll credit you.
ComplaintSo sorry about this, [name]. We've sent you a DM so we can sort it out properly.

UGC permission flow

  1. Find: search #AeroRunFitCheck and tags daily
  2. Ask: comment the permission request (saved reply 'Feature request')
  3. Confirm: only feature after a written 'Yes'; screenshot and log it
  4. Credit: tag the creator in the post and first line of the caption
  5. Thank: DM a thank-you; top fits get a story shout-out
Strategist's note

Decision. Never feature customer content without a written 'yes', and never invent reviews or testimonials.

Alternatives considered. Reposting tagged posts automatically (common, and quicker).

Why. A tag isn't consent. Asking first protects the brand legally, and people are more likely to say yes and share the repost when they've been asked.
