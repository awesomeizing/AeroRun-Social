# AeroRun: Reel Scripts

8 Reels for the 30-day campaign. Independent fictional concept, not an official Nike ad.

**Format rules for every Reel:** 9:16, 1080 × 1920; product or hook on screen within the first second; captions burned in (many people watch muted); keep text out of the bottom ~320 px and top ~220 px (Instagram UI). Creator Reels are posted as **Paid partnership** with an ASCI disclosure label (#ad) in the caption, not just the platform tag.
